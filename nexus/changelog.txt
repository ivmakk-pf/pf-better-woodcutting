Added: Stump removal: with an axe in hand, hold the interact key at a tree stump to remove it and get the Wood of one log.

Fixed: The hit assist helps from your first swing at a tree. Before, your first swings in a game session could get no help.
Fixed: When two trees fall at almost the same moment, the logs of each tree go to the player who cut that tree.
